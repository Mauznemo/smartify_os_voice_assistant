## 0.1.0

- First version: a wake word or a button, Parakeet, Whisper or Moonshine to listen,
  Kokoro or Piper to answer, all on the car. Commands by phrase or by words, built-in
  ones for the volume, the music, apps and the time, and an optional AI server and
  speech server.
- One page that downloads everything the picked models need, a chime when it starts
  and stops listening, answers that sound like a person, and a wake word that is heard
  far more reliably, with a debug view of what it hears.
- Teach it your voice: say the wake word eight times and it learns how you say it.
- The waves show on light themes too.
